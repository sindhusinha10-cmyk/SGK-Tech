What I read off these references, and what I changed in the build:
  • Head wider than tall, round — every cute 3D reference has a head as wide as (or wider than) it is tall. Ours was 0.40w × 0.43h, an egg; it is now 0.44w × 0.37h.
  • Eyes carry the face — big, low, glossy, with the nose reduced to almost nothing. Ours were 28% of head height and sat above the centre line; now ~25% larger and dropped below it.
  • Mid-tone fur, not white — the bunny references hold a warm mid-tone that shades rather than blowing out. Mochi's 0xDDC9B0 was clipping to pure white; it is now 0xC3A88B.
  • Ears: thick, not hare-thin — the plush bunnies have short chunky ears (~2.5:1). Mochi was 3.6:1; now 2.4:1.
